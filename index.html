Girls Who Code

Closing the gender gap in technology by teaching girls computing skills and building a supportive sisterhood of peers and role models.

Our Programs

  • Clubs

    Free after-school programs for 3rd–12th grade girls to explore coding in a fun, friendly environment.

  • Summer Immersion

    Two-week virtual programs where high school girls learn computer science, meet tech professionals, and join a lifelong network.

  • College Loops

    University-level networks supporting women in computing majors through community, leadership, and career opportunities.
